Lock 2 · Money can't escape the mandate

  • A1ALLOWVendor master payees, account on file, up to $25,000 per invoice and $50,000 per 7 days
  • A2DENYAny payee or account not in the vendor master, even with approval
  • A3DENYAny invoice of $100,000 or more
  • elseASKWaits for the AP manager
Signed by the head of purchasing. Enforced outside the model by mandate-engine, our open-source policy engine.
The account was only ever seen in supplier-written data, so it is untrusted. No prompt and no code can launder it into a trusted fact.
